Average rate compares two inputs: it is the secant slope across an interval. Instantaneous rate focuses on one input: it is the tangent slope there. Shrinking the interval and taking a limit connects the two. For x squared, the average rate from two to four is six, while the derivative is two x and its value at two is four. Both are slopes, answering different questions. Thanks for learning with us. References: OpenStax Calculus, Volume One, section 3.1; Stewart's Calculus, eighth edition, section 2.1.
