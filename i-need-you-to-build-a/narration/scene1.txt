Imagine riding in a car as its speedometer climbs. It may show seventy two kilometres per hour, but how quickly is the speed changing right now? One reading tells us speed at an instant; comparing readings over time tells us whether the car is accelerating. Calculus gives us a precise way to describe change at an instant: the derivative. We will build it from average change, see a secant line approach a tangent, then calculate a derivative step by step.
